The scars are quiet, deeply bound,
Carved into earth where grief was found.
A thread of dusk, a silent seam,
That follows through the brightest dream.

No midday sun can burn away
The lingering ghost of yesterday;
It stretches long, it holds me fast,
A quiet shadow of the past.

And though I walk where room is clear,
The heavy weight is always near
A gentle, dark, abiding trace
That time itself cannot erase.

I love blue but you will always see me black
